Film & campaign · Motion · 2025

Atrium Arts

The brief.

Atrium needed a season campaign that could fill a city and still feel like a quiet gallery. We shot one long film across the building at night and cut it into everything — street posters, cinema spots and a forty-second loop for the lobby.

What it did in market.

Measured across the full season, against the same period the year before.

Visitors

Season attendance up 28%, with first-time visitors making up nearly half of all tickets sold.

+28%

Reach

The campaign film was screened before features in eleven independent cinemas across the region.

11 venues

Members

New annual memberships during the run were the highest in the gallery's recorded history.

+1,900

Reuse

The same footage has since been recut by their team for two further exhibitions.

2 recuts
